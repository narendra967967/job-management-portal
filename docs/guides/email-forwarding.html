Why forward your job alerts

You already get job alerts by email — from LinkedIn, Naukri and other job sites. Forward them to your private Jobtiva address and every job in them becomes a lead within seconds. Jobtiva never opens your mailbox: it only sees the emails you forward.

Set it up in Gmail (once, about 2 minutes)

  1. Copy your address
    Open Settings → Email forwarding and click Copy address.
  2. Add it in Gmail
    In Gmail, open Settings (the gear) → See all settings → Forwarding and POP/IMAP → Add a forwarding address. Paste your address, then Next → Proceed.
  3. Confirm it
    Within a few seconds a Confirm forwarding button appears on your Email forwarding card. Click it, approve on Google's page, then come back and click I've confirmed it. Leave Gmail's own forwarding option on Disable forwarding — the filter in the next step does the forwarding.
  4. Create the filter
    In Gmail's search bar, open Show search options. In From, paste the senders line from your card (use its Copy button). Click Create filter, tick Forward it to, choose your Jobtiva address, then Create filter.

Check it's working

  • Your Email forwarding card says Working and shows when the last alert arrived.
  • New jobs appear on Leads, labelled with the site they came from.
  • Anything that couldn't be read is listed under Settings → Ingestion issues.

Gmail filters only forward new email, so your first leads arrive with the next alert. Creating or editing a job alert on the job site usually sends one soon.

Which job sites work

Alerts from the job sites listed on your card become leads automatically. Emails from other senders are ignored. If you add your own AI key (Settings → AI provider), Jobtiva can also read alerts from supported sites it can't read on its own.

Work or school account?

Some organisations block automatic forwarding. Forward an alert by hand instead — open it, click Forward and send it to your Jobtiva address — from the email you sign in with, or the Gmail you set forwarding up from. You can also use Add with AI on the Leads page.

Keep your address private

Job alerts sent to it become leads in your account. If it's shared by mistake, use Reset on your Email forwarding card — the old address stops working at once. Then update the address in your Gmail filter.

Stop forwarding

Delete the filter in Gmail (Settings → Filters and Blocked Addresses). Your leads stay in Jobtiva.
