Besides the Gmail filter, a user can forward a single alert by hand: open it in Gmail, press Forward and send it to their Jobtiva address. This is how they get an old alert read again, for example after adding an AI key.

## When it's accepted

- It comes from the user's **sign-in email**, or from the **Gmail address they confirmed forwarding with**, and is signed by that address's domain.
- The **original alert** can be found inside it, as an attached email or quoted in the body.
- That original is from an **allowed job site**.

The jobs are then read from the original, exactly as if the filter had sent it. In the Email log the reason starts with "Hand-forwarded ·".

## When it isn't

| Reason | What to do |
|---|---|
| Hand-forwarded · original sender not found | Forward the original alert itself, not a forward of a forward |
| Hand-forwarded from a site we don't accept yet | The site isn't allowed yet; **Ask us to add it** |
| Hand-forwarded · already processed | That exact forward came in before |

Forwarding from any other address, like a work email, is rejected as a sender we don't accept.
