Every lead sits in one stage. Five are open, meaning the job is still being worked: **New**, **Shortlisted**, **Applied**, **Interview** and **Offer Received**. Two are final: **Closed** and **Discarded**. Only open leads take reminders, to-dos and outreach.

## What each list shows

| List | What's in it |
|---|---|
| Leads | Every open lead that isn't stale, as a list or a board |
| Ready to action | Shortlisted and Applied leads that aren't stale. Nothing else is checked: no description or contact needed |
| Outreached | Open leads with a message marked as sent. A saved draft doesn't count |
| Archive | Closed, discarded and stale leads |

An Applied lead with a sent message shows up in both Ready to action and Outreached. That's intended.

## Moving a lead along

The opened lead has a button beside the job link that moves it one stage forward, such as **Mark as Applied** on a Shortlisted lead. It's colored like the stage it leads to, and it disappears at Offer Received, because closing a job needs a reason. To skip a stage or close a job, use **Change status** in the ⋯ menu.

Leads also move on their own. Some moves follow what the user does, such as saving a description, and some come from the daily cleanup rules. Both are covered in their own articles.

## Top Choice

The star beside the title marks a lead as a Top Choice. The card turns gold, a filter shows only these, and the daily cleanup rules never touch them. Starring a New lead moves it to Shortlisted.

## The board

On a laptop, Leads can show a board with a column per stage. Dragging a card to Closed asks for the reason first. The board doesn't appear on phones or tablets, which get the list instead.
