A lead goes stale when all of these are true:

- It's **New, Shortlisted or Applied**. Interview and Offer Received leads never go stale, since they're clearly active.
- It has **no pending reminder**.
- **Nothing has happened on it** for longer than the user's limit, set in Settings → Follow-ups under "Flag lead as stale after (days)". The default is 14.

"Something happened" means the latest of: the lead being captured, the user changing it (stage, Top Choice, edits), saving its description, adding a contact, or an alert listing the same job again.

## Where stale leads go

They leave Leads, Ready to action, Outreached, the board and the New count in the top bar, and appear only in Archive with a Stale badge. They're still open, nothing is deleted, and they come back as soon as something happens on them. Changing the stage, adding a reminder, or a new alert for the job all bring one back.

This is the answer to "my lead disappeared": check Archive.

## The warning before it happens

Cards warn as a lead gets close. The border turns gray in steps as the lead uses up 30%, 50%, 70% and 90% of the stale period with no activity, and from 70% a chip says "Stale in N days", or "Stale tomorrow" on the last day. Any activity resets the clock and clears the warning.

## Stale isn't the end

A stale lead can still be closed by the daily cleanup rules later: a New lead with no activity for 30 days, for example. See the article on automatic cleanup.
