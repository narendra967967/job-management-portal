| Feature | Where | Needs |
|---|---|---|
| Save & Summarize with AI | A lead's job description | Key |
| Structure with AI | Adding a contact from pasted text | Key |
| Fit score | A lead, scored against a chosen résumé | Key |
| Add with AI | Add lead, from pasted text or a screenshot | Key, within the plan's monthly limit |
| Draft with AI | Outreach on a lead | Key, plus the AI outreach drafts plan switch |
| Estimate my salary | A lead's Overview | Key, plus the AI salary insight plan switch |
| Résumé ATS check | Each résumé | Key, the Résumé ATS score check plan switch, and a complete career profile |
| AI reading of alerts | Forwarded alerts from sites without a reader | Key, plus the AI reads alerts plan switch |
| Custom prompts | Settings → AI prompts | The custom prompts plan switch |

## The career profile

**Settings → Career profile** feeds the fit score and outreach drafts: target roles, title, years of experience, seniority, skills, tools, industries, work mode and locations. Drafts also get the professional summary and the selected résumé's text. Pay, work eligibility and the optional diversity questions never go to AI. Changing the profile doesn't change existing fit scores until the lead is rescored.

The ATS check needs the profile complete: title, years, at least 3 skills, at least one target role, a summary and at least one job. Until then it shows how complete the profile is.

## Scoring and stage moves

Scoring a lead can move it to Shortlisted. While the Strong fit cleanup rule is on, only a score at or above its number does (80 by default). See the article on automatic stage moves.
