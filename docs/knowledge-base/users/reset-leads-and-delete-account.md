Both live in the danger zone of **Settings → Account**, and both are confirmed with a 6-digit code emailed to the user. A code lasts 10 minutes and allows 5 tries, and a new one can be sent after 60 seconds.

## Reset leads

This deletes all of the user's leads, contacts, outreach, reminders and to-dos, along with the automatic cleanup history and lead notifications. It keeps their account and plan, profile and career profile, résumés, settings and AI key, forwarding address and Gmail connection, notes, support tickets and Email log.

Old alerts aren't read again afterward, because we still remember which emails were processed. Before resetting, the user can download their leads as a CSV. They type RESET, enter the code, and everything goes at once, followed by a confirmation email.

## Request account deletion

The user can't delete their account themselves. They send a request, with an optional reason, and confirm it with a code. It arrives in admin Tickets as an Account deletion ticket. Their account keeps working, and Settings → Account shows when they asked, with a **Cancel request** button. Canceling sends them a thank-you email.

An admin carries out the deletion from the ticket, or from Users. It removes everything, résumé files included, and deletes the user's other tickets. The deletion ticket is kept, anonymized, as a record. If the user has canceled, the admin is asked to confirm before deleting anyway, and bulk deletes skip them.
