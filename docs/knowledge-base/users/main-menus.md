The top of the user sidebar has two main menus. Clicking one opens its first page and lists its own menus underneath.

| Main menu | Opens on | Menus under it |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead management** | Leads | Leads, Ready to action, Outreached, Archive, To-do, Reminders, Contacts, Outreach (all the menus users had before) |
| **Mock interviews** | AI mock interviews | AI mock interviews, Human interviews, Interview history, Performance |

Settings, Help & support, How-to guides and notifications belong to neither. When a user opens one of those, the sidebar keeps showing whichever main menu they were last in.

On phones, the two main menus are at the top of the menu that opens from the top-left button. The bottom bar follows the main menu that's open: lead pages inside Lead management, the four interview pages inside Mock interviews.

Inside Mock interviews, the Add lead button, the lead search and the lead counts in the top bar are hidden, since they only make sense for leads.

## Mock interviews is Coming soon for now

Every Mock interviews page currently shows a short description and a **Coming soon** label. Nothing can be started or bought there yet. The features arrive in stages, with admin switches to open each part when it's ready.

## When a plan ends

Mock interviews isn't part of the plan lock. A user in limited mode (plan ended) sees Lead management locked, as before, but can still open Mock interviews. Their bottom bar on a phone has an extra Interviews tab for it.
